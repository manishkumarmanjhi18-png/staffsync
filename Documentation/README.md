## 📑 Summary of Phases

### 1. [Ideation Phase](Ideation%20Phase-(1)/Ideation%20Phase)
* **Brainstorm & Idea Prioritization:** Identifies core operational bottlenecks in HR and prioritizes high-impact features (RBAC, digital shift punch, automated leave approvals).
* **Customer Problem Statements:** Defines explicit problem statements for HR personnel, department managers, and employees.
* **Empathy Map Canvas:** Visualizes what organizational stakeholders Hear, Think & Feel, See, Say, and Do, along with identified Pains and Gains.

### 2. [Project Planning Phase](Project%20Planning%20Phase(2))
* **Product Backlog & Sprint Schedule:** 4 distinct sprints covering User Authentication (Sprint 1), Employee Directory (Sprint 2), Attendance & Leaves (Sprint 3), and Corporate Communications & Training (Sprint 4).
* **Velocity & Estimation:** 80 total story points (20 points per 6-day sprint).

### 3. [Requirement Analysis](Requirement%20Analysis(3))
* **Functional & Non-Functional Requirements:** Details 6 core functional epics and 6 non-functional requirements (Usability, Security, Reliability, Performance, Availability, Scalability).
* **Technology Stack:** Details the 3-tier MERN stack (React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB).
* **Data Flow Diagram (DFD):** DFD Level 0 and Level 1 mapping data flows between users, controllers, and MongoDB collections.

### 4. [Project Design Phase](Project%20Design%20Phase(4))
* **Problem – Solution Fit:** Validates how StaffSync eliminates manual paperwork and spreadsheet errors.
* **Proposed Solution:** Technical description, novelty parameters, social impact, and revenue model.
* **Solution Architecture:** Decoupled client-server architecture with strict MVC design pattern.

### 5. [Performance Testing & UAT](Performance%20Testing)
* **User Acceptance Testing (UAT):** Comprehensive test cases (TC-001 through TC-008) covering positive and negative flows, bug tracking, and sign-off.
